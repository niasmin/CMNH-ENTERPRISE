# Care & Beyond — original warm low-poly 3D

User-selected direction: actual warm low-poly 3D, with readable models and characters. This supersedes the proposed illustrated sprite atlases; no image-generation jobs were started.

All default game meshes, furnishings, character rigs, landscape, props and icons are newly authored procedural geometry/SVG. No old JPEG atlas, old character sprite or old building PNG is used by the rebuilt default renderer. Game economy and save keys are retained.

Palette: porcelain ivory, sage/mint lawns, deep pine structure, muted terracotta/coral accents, teal/blue nursing uniforms, honey daylight. Orthographic orbit camera, open cutaway rooms, soft contact shadows, flat shaded foliage and readable silhouettes.

Ten facility models reflect existing core building types. Room sets include beds and privacy dividers, therapy equipment, activity tables, home-care reception, training seats, VIP suite, hospital wards, lab benches and management offices. Staff/elder models use grouped editable limbs, wheelchair variants and programmatic idle/walk/run/care poses. Asset thumbnails and UI portrait renders are made from the same models used in the world.

Three.js is vendored locally from a pinned npm release, preserving its MIT license and validating npm tarball integrity. Game-specific model geometry is authored in this repository, not downloaded from a provider. No paid provider jobs or third-party asset licensing receipts are claimed.
